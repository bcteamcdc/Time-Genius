Weekly Management App for Kids

This software was created by the coding club association of Gampaha Bandaranayake College, its narrow purpose is a time management software to allocate time for their educational work with the new world.

Priority (1-10) Explanation

You can give 5 to 10 transits to the subject you want to prioritize and schedule more time for it.

You can study less and give a lower priority to the subject, that is between 1 and 5 and schedule the time according to the number you have given.
